# Support

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/KSAGlory/KSA-Cinematic-Tools/issues) for reproducible bugs or installation questions. Include your MTA version, the command used, and relevant sanitized debug output. Do not include passwords, account databases, IP addresses, or tokens.

The project is a small chat-command tool, not a turnkey cinematic gamemode or NPC AI system.
